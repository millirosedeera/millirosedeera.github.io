Hermes Google Workspace

A personal Google Workspace integration used by its account owner for productivity and job-search workflows.

Purpose

The application can, when authorized, access Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar, Contacts, Docs and Sheets to help the owner review correspondence, manage information, and organize related tasks. Its use is limited to the features and permissions that the owner configures.

Account authorization

Google access is granted by the account owner through OAuth. Access may be revoked through Google Account settings.
