Privacy Policy

Updated November 4, 2026

Hermes Google Workspace is a personal-use integration configured by its account owner.

Data accessed

Depending on the Google OAuth permissions granted and the workflows used, the integration may access email messages and metadata, email sending and modification capabilities, contacts, Google Drive files, Google Calendar information, and Google Docs and Sheets content.

Purpose

The account owner uses this information to perform personal productivity and recruitment-related tasks, including reviewing communications and documents, organizing schedules, and managing related work.

Processing and storage

Data and OAuth credentials may be processed or stored by the owner's configured local automation software and any cloud or AI providers the owner chooses to use. Actual storage, transfers, and retention depend on the configuration of those tools and providers. This notice does not claim that all information stays on the owner's device.

Access and control

The owner can review or revoke access in Google Account security settings. Revoking OAuth access prevents new authorized requests but does not automatically delete previously stored local files or copies held by other processors. The owner is responsible for reviewing configured providers and removing unnecessary data.

Scope

This page describes a privately operated integration and does not imply Google endorsement or security certification.
